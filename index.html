Visit Lesotho, The Mountain Kingdom

I made this site to help visitors see Lesotho. We have high mountains, cold snow in winter, and big waterfalls. Most people come for hiking and fresh air.

Places I Recommend

  1. Semonkong, home of Maletsunyane Falls
  2. Sani Pass, on the border with South Africa
  3. Katse Dam, one of the biggest dams in Africa
  4. Thaba Bosiu, where Moshoeshoe lived
